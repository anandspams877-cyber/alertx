Limitations: DISASTER AI uses transparent computer-vision heuristics running locally in your browser (colour/flicker analysis for fire and smoke, texture + colour analysis for water, frame-to-frame motion matching for shaking). No video leaves your device. It is not a trained neural network and can be wrong: orange objects, grey walls, blue surfaces or a shaky hand can trigger false alarms. Earthquake detection with a normal webcam is unreliable because a moved or bumped camera looks identical. Results are estimates, never confirmed disasters, and no emergency service is contacted.
